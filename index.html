What you get

      
One prompt box. A full stack behind it.

      
AI Dream Builder isn't a code snippet generator. It plans, writes, tests, and deploys — across apps, games, and internal tools.

    

    

      

        
⚡

        
Any AI model, one box

        
Switch between Claude, GPT, and Gemini per project, or bring your own API key. AI Dream Builder routes the prompt, you keep control.

      

      

        
🕹️

        
Apps and games

        
From CRUD dashboards to 2D game loops with physics and sprites — the builder isn't limited to forms and tables.

      

      

        
🧩

        
Real, editable code

        
Every build is a real repo. Open the file tree, edit by hand, or hand the diff back to AI Dream Builder to keep iterating.

      

      

        
🚀

        
One-click deploy

        
Ship straight to a live URL with hosting, environment variables, and previews handled for you.

      

      

        
💳

        
Payments built in

        
Drop in Stripe checkout and subscriptions without wiring a backend yourself — AI Dream Builder scaffolds it.

      

      

        
🔒

        
Your data, your infra

        
Export any project, connect your own database, or self-host the generated app. Nothing is locked in.

      

    

  






  

    

      
The flow

      
From idea to live URL, in three steps

    

    

      

        01
        
Describe the build

        
Tell AI Dream Builder what you're making and pick a model. No boilerplate, no starter templates to fight with.

      

      

        02
        
Watch it get built

        03
